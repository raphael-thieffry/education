## My work

**Mazes**: [`my-work/mazes/`](my-work/mazes/) · Python, Jupyter · team project

A small library to generate and study perfect mazes, modelled as connected acyclic graphs: generation by randomised exhaustive exploration, enumeration of small mazes, pseudo-mazes versus mazes. [`Diapo.ipynb`](my-work/mazes/Diapo.ipynb) is the final presentation.

Labs: [`my-work/labs/`](my-work/labs/) (recursive paths, dice games, …).
